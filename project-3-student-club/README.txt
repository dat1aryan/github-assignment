=== Shoolini Tech & Innovation Club ===
Welcome to the Student Club Management Repository.
This repository tracks club activities, member lists, and upcoming events.

Club Purpose:
To bridge the gap between academic curriculum and practical industry skills.

Club Objectives:
1. Conduct weekly coding and version control workshops.
2. Organize annual hackathons and tech symposiums.
3. Build open-source community projects.
4. Mentor junior students in software engineering practices.
